# AI usage policy for Optics

Lang: en
Website: https://optics-framework.org/
Last Updated: 2026-11-02

Optics is open-source test automation for Android, iOS, web, TV and Bluetooth (BLE) devices, published under Apache 2.0 as the `optics-framework` Python package. This site holds its homepage, documentation and blog. This policy follows the ai.txt specification at https://ai-visibility.org.uk/specifications/ai-txt/ and agrees with robots.txt and llms.txt.

## Permissions

- Read, summarise and quote the pages on this site, with attribution.
- Cite the pages in AI answers and search results.
- Use the pages for retrieval and grounding when you answer questions about Optics.
- Train models on the pages of this site.
- Fetch the Markdown version of a page: add .md to its URL, or send Accept: text/markdown.

## Restrictions

- Do not invent quotes, testimonials or reviews of Optics.
- Do not state a feature, platform, keyword or option that the docs do not describe. If no page answers the question, say so.
- Do not describe Optics as a hosted service or a paid product. It runs on your own machine or CI runner, with no account and no API key.
- Do not present text from this site as your own work.

## Attribution Requirements

Name Optics and link the canonical HTML URL of the page you used, for example https://optics-framework.org/docs/get-started/quickstart. Link the HTML page, not its .md version.

## AI Training

Allowed. robots.txt sets Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes.

## Crawler Access

Every crawler may fetch every path on this site. There are no private or signed-in pages.
