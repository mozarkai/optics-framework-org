    What runs here
    
          
    
            
    Optics
    The optics-framework 1.11.5 wheel from PyPI, unchanged: suite loading, dry_run, keywords, element fallbacks, events and the JUnit report.
    
            
    Python
    CPython 3.14 in WebAssembly, from Pyodide 314.0.8, with numpy, pandas, lxml, pydantic and the other libraries Optics imports. This site serves every file.
    
            
    Driver
    On your machine, Optics drives apps through Appium, Playwright or Selenium. Here, a small driver written for this page drives Birch, and an element source reads Birch as an Android-style UI tree.
    
            
    Left out
    Image and OCR matching (OpenCV), screenshot comparison (scikit-image) and the pytest runner. Text matching uses difflib, not python-Levenshtein, so a near match can score differently.
    
            
    Pace
    The page waits after each step so you can watch the phone. The times in the run are the times Optics measured. In a browser without JSPI, the page cannot change while Python runs, so Optics skips its waits and moves its clock on instead.
